## evtFunc11 (code_r1_002F59D0, raidou1 0x200, raidou2 0x200)

Raidou 1 and Raidou 2: 64 of 128 words differ, first at +0x24.

Retail copies the 8-byte size constant with ldl/ldr before the tables; mine loads two floats. Unsized extern avoids gp addressing.
